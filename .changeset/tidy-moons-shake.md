Sparklines can now plot a different entity than the tile itself — show the living room temperature with outdoor humidity underneath it, or total energy with live power. You can also pick which statistic the sparkline draws (mean, min, max, sum, state or change), which finally gives energy meters a sparkline at all - Home Assistant never records a mean for them, so they have always come up blank. Both are available per entity and in the visual editor, which no longer loses per-entity settings when you edit the entity list.
